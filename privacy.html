Privacy Policy

Creator Post Studio — Effective date: 18 November 2026

1. What we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we receive basic profile information through TikTok's official Login Kit: your TikTok username, display name, and profile picture. When you publish a video, we process the video file and caption you provide. We also keep minimal technical logs needed to operate the service.

2. How we use it

Your TikTok profile information is used only to show you which account is connected, so you can confirm the correct account before publishing. Your video files and captions are used only to publish them to your own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API, as you instruct.

3. What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties, except with TikTok's API as technically required to publish your videos. We do not access your followers' data, your messages, or any TikTok account other than the one you connect.

4. Data retention

Video files you upload are deleted after the publishing attempt is complete. Your connected TikTok account information is kept only while your account remains connected. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok app under Settings > Security > Manage apps, or by contacting us.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time by contacting us at the email address below.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
